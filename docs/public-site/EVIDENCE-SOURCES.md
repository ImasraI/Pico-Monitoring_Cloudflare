# Evidence library provenance

Verified 2026-10-09 against NLM PubMed records and abstracts, fetched in one batch through NCBI EFetch. Summaries are abstract-based; no claim of full-text review for every entry. The DentalMonitoring Evidence Hub was consulted for discovery, not used as the source of study findings. This is a curated library, not a systematic review of all literature.

The library includes independent teleorthodontics/communication research as well as system-specific DentalMonitoring studies. Reviews may include the primary studies listed here: entries are not independent replications and their counts must not be interpreted as cumulative proof.

All Pico relevance values are field-context: no documented direct design link or product-specific clinical validation was supplied. Ongoing Iranian PM research wording is user-supplied; no unpublished study details or results are represented.

Publication year follows the journal issue in PubMed (including Sangalli 2024, online 2023; Byrne 2021, online 2020). Original titles and full author lists come from NLM. Journal names omit NLM's long descriptive subtitle where present.

## Scientific boundaries

- Small prospective hygiene/retention studies do not establish broad prevention effects.
- The Lam randomized trial reports fewer visits **and longer treatment**.
- Nonsignificant PAR differences do not establish equivalence.
- Morris 2019 uses typodonts, not patients. Moylan compares 30 measurement sets, not 30 patients.
- Diagnostic studies evaluate a particular algorithm against an expert reference; they do not establish fewer real-world emergencies or PM accuracy.
- Satisfaction surveys measure respondents' perceptions, not clinical efficacy.
- The 2026 aligner study discloses financial relationships with DentalMonitoring; the hygiene study discloses partial company sponsorship.
