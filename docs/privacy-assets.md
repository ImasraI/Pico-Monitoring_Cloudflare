## Final prompts

One built-in generation call was used per selected image. Two initial concepts were replaced with simpler illustrations to avoid apparent software interfaces or unrelated medical imagery. The selected prompt set follows.

### privacy-hero-concept

Create a premium minimalist healthcare privacy illustration for a modern remote orthodontic monitoring brand. Use a deep powerful blue gradient, subtle white highlights, a translucent protective shield, refined abstract medical data streams, and a discreet visual reference to digital dental care. Sophisticated 3D editorial illustration, realistic material rendering, balanced negative space, clean composition, premium technology brand aesthetic, soft studio lighting. Wide landscape composition, 16:9 aspect ratio. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no padlock stock icon, no fake certification badge. The illustration should communicate responsible healthcare data management rather than an absolute cybersecurity guarantee.

### privacy-image-processing-concept

Create a restrained, premium healthcare editorial 3D illustration, wide 16:9 landscape. Three simple sculptural concepts on a white and very pale blue studio surface: on the right, a smartphone shown ONLY from its plain back (no display); at center, one ceramic schematic dental arch surrounded by two empty translucent image-framing planes and subtle alignment guides; on the left, a small abstract human silhouette representing a clinician, without identifiable facial features, studying the physical dental arch. Fine blue connecting light lines gently relate the three objects. Calm and sparse, accurate understated dental-care reference, clean blue-white palette, no brain or neural network cliché. CRITICAL: no screens showing anything, no computer monitor, no UI, no app interface, no document panels, no fake clinical images, no patient records, no graphs, no text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, badges, diagnosis symbols or claims of automatic diagnosis. This is a conceptual illustration of image capture, preparation and clinician review, not an image of an actual product.

### privacy-family-concept

Create a warm but professional healthcare editorial illustration showing a parent or guardian accompanying an adolescent in an ordinary home setting while using a smartphone for orthodontic care. The scene should emphasize responsible support, privacy, trust, and family participation without depicting real identifiable patients. Use a restrained deep-blue and light-neutral palette consistent with a premium orthodontic technology brand. Natural lighting, realistic proportions, calm composition, subtle technology context, suitable for a professional healthcare website. Landscape 3:2 composition. No text, logos, visible personal medical records, watermark, exaggerated expressions, or childish cartoon style.

### privacy-data-lifecycle-concept

Create one very minimalist conceptual data-lifecycle editorial illustration in a wide 16:9 landscape composition on a white or very light blue background. Exactly three abstract sculptural elements connected by a thin flowing blue line with a few understated nodes: on the right, a small group of plain translucent square tiles entering the path; in the center, the same tiles aligned in a calm glass enclosure with an open top; on the left, a plain closed neutral archival folio at the path's endpoint. Blue glass and white ceramic, soft studio light, generous negative space, clean subtle proportions. Represent information entering, being managed during a service relationship and reaching an appropriate retention endpoint. CRITICAL: no people, medical scenes, organs, lungs, hearts, readouts, charts, text, letters, numbers, fake interfaces, data tables, locks, shields, deletion/trash/shredder icons, security badges or certification marks. Do not imply automatic deletion or guaranteed anonymization. Pure abstract material metaphor, premium healthcare-technology aesthetic.
